How long have you not had a conversation with the past you, even though the previous patterns and habits keep multiplying within? Perhaps, memory isn't a container, it's a pipe. Breathe the past from it, hear every remorse, bliss, nostalgia, or numbness manifesting itself through sound again. Am I clinging to some? Am I singing in a choir of my own echoes all this time? Should I prolong the duration of this Self? Should I break it?

_You, the Duration_ is a sonic improvisation performed on [CCRMA Open House 2026 Concert](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/ccrma-open-house-2026). Through real-time audio feature extraction, the machine retrieves previously sampled audio clips based on their similarity to the current microphone input, and recombines them into an output stream. Just like how memory and behavior condition each other, this mechanism closes an auditory feedback loop where the input becomes the output, delayed and altered, and again shapes the next input.

---

The pre-recorded performance and the sound installation version was presented in [Stanford Winter 2026 Open Studios](https://events.stanford.edu/event/winter-2026-open-studios).

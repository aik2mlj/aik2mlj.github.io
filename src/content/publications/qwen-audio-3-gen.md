Technical report, arXiv, July 2026

Qwen-Audio-3.0-Gen-Preview is a unified generative model for long-form, multi-character audio scenes with explicit temporal control. I co-developed it during my research internship at Qwen Audio, Alibaba, where I also worked on the semantic alignment of its RVQ-based audio tokenizer and on curating the training data.

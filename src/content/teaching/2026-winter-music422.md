I served as a teaching assistant for Prof. Marina Bosi in [Music 422](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/courses/422), Winter 2026. The course covers the theory and practice of perceptual audio coding, and students build a working audio codec over the quarter.

I taught a review lecture on perceptual audio codec components, and modernized the coding environment through a reusable [template repository](https://github.com/aik2mlj/MUSIC422-template).
